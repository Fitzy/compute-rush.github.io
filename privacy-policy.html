Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Compute Rush: Idle Clicker is a free idle game. Data processing is handled by third-party services as described below. We use Google Firebase for anonymous gameplay analytics and to store voluntarily submitted feedback.

Advertising

This app displays ads via Google AdMob, with Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) as additional ad networks through AdMob mediation and bidding. These services may automatically collect or receive information such as IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, device and app information, approximate location, and ad interaction or performance data. They may use this information to select and deliver ads, provide personalised advertising where permitted, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and improve their services. This is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Liftoff Privacy Policy

Data We Collect

When you use Google Play Games Services features (leaderboards, achievements), the following data is collected and shared with Google to provide these features:

  • Player ID – your unique Google Play Games identifier, used to associate your progress across sessions
  • Gamer profile – your public display name and avatar, used to identify you on leaderboards
  • Achievements – achievement unlock status and progress, used to track and display your in-game accomplishments
  • Leaderboard data – scores submitted to leaderboards, used to rank players competitively
  • Play activity – gameplay sessions and activity metadata, used to support Play Games features and improve the experience
  • Saved game data – your game progress (a save file containing your in-game state), transmitted off your device and stored in your account's Play Games cloud save storage, used to back up and synchronise your progress across devices

This data is processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. No account registration is required to play the core game, but signing in to Google Play Games is required to use leaderboards, achievements, and cloud saves.

Cloud Saves

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your game progress can be saved to the cloud using Google Play Games Services. When this happens, a copy of your save file is transmitted off your device to Google and stored in your account's hidden Play Games application data folder in Google Drive, associated with your Google account identifier (Player ID). This allows your progress to be backed up and restored, including on other devices where you sign in to the same account.

We do not have access to the contents of your cloud save data; it is stored and managed by Google on your behalf and tied to your Google account. Because the save data is transmitted off your device and linked to your account, it is no longer stored solely on your device. You can play without signing in to Google Play Games, in which case your progress is saved only on your device and is not transmitted to the cloud.

Analytics Data

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data that helps us understand how players interact with the game and improve the experience. This data is collected automatically and includes:

  • Gameplay events – actions such as purchasing servers, hiring engineers, unlocking tiers, and watching ads
  • Session data – session start/end times and session duration
  • Device information – device model, operating system version, and screen resolution
  • App information – app version and crash reports

This data is anonymous and cannot be used to identify you personally. No personal information, gameplay saves, or account details are included in analytics data.

Feedback Data

When you voluntarily submit feedback through the in-app feedback form, the following data is collected and stored in Google Firebase:

  • Feedback category and message – the category you select and the text you write
  • Device information – device model and Android OS version, used to help diagnose reported issues
  • App version – the version of the app you are using
  • Player ID and display name – your Google Play Games identifier and display name, if signed in at the time of submission

This data is collected solely to improve the game based on player feedback. No feedback data is collected unless you choose to submit it.

Game progress is saved locally on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your progress may also be saved to the cloud as described under "Cloud Saves" above.

Data Retention & Deletion

Local game save data is stored on your device and can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Data associated with Google Play Games Services (player profile, achievements, leaderboard scores, play activity, and cloud save data) is retained by Google in accordance with their data retention policies. You can manage or delete this data through your Google Account settings at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. Cloud save data is stored in your Play Games application data folder and can be deleted by clearing your Play Games saved game data for this app, or by disconnecting the app from Google Play Games via your Google Account, which revokes the app's access to your player data.

Anonymous analytics data is retained by Google Firebase for up to 14 months, after which it is automatically deleted.

Feedback data submitted through the in-app feedback form is retained in Google Firebase for up to 12 months, after which it is deleted. You can request deletion of your feedback data at any time by contacting us via the Google Play Store listing.

Advertising data collected by Google AdMob, Unity Ads, and Liftoff Monetize is retained and managed by Google, Unity Technologies, and Liftoff respectively, in accordance with their privacy policies. You can manage your Google ad personalisation preferences at adssettings.google.com. For information about Unity Ads data, refer to the Unity Privacy Policy. For information about Liftoff's retention practices and privacy choices, refer to the Liftoff Privacy Policy and Liftoff opt-out page.
