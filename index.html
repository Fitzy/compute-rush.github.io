Frequently Asked Questions

Is Compute Rush free to play?

Yes, Compute Rush is completely free to download and play on Android. You can watch optional ads to boost your progress, but spending money is never required.

How do offline earnings work?

Your data centers continue generating income even when the app is closed. When you return, you'll collect all the earnings your servers produced while you were away.

What kind of game is Compute Rush?

Compute Rush is an idle tycoon clicker game. Tap to earn, hire engineers to automate, and invest in upgrades to build a data center empire that earns money for you over time, even when you're not playing.

Where can I download Compute Rush?

Compute Rush is available on the Google Play Store for Android devices.
